Windows Playtest M36.4

Learn explorer controls, companion summoning, combat, missions, homesteads and local save recovery.

Download WorldOpen Web Wiki

M36.4 is a Windows development playtest. World saves are local; Web accounts and cloud progress do not transfer automatically.

Overview

Player handbook for the published Primalbound World/Desktop M36.4 Windows playtest. This is the 3D human-explorer RPG, not the browser auto-battler. The game, progression rules, currencies and save files differ from Primalbound Web. Last reviewed 26 October 2026 against the M36.4 source and release notes. Screens and exact unlock labels may still change during development.

Download the M36.4 Windows playtest

_Use the numbered section headings below to navigate._

1. Install, launch and update #

  1. From the verified release above, download the release asset named PrimalboundWorld-Windows.zip.
  2. Extract the entire ZIP into a normal folder. Do not launch files directly from inside the ZIP.
  3. Run PrimalboundLauncher.exe. You do not need to install the Godot editor.
  4. Select PLAY CURRENT VERSION to keep playing the installed build.
  5. If the launcher offers a newer verified Stable build, UPDATE & PLAY is optional. The updater stages and verifies releases; it is not supposed to overwrite your local campaign saves.
  6. Read the included PLAYTEST.txt for the exact installed build's known limits and any version-specific control changes.

The public M36.4 release is a development playtest, not a finished Steam game. Keep the entire extracted folder together and retain an exported JSON backup before testing upgrades or recovery actions.

2. Your first session — recommended route #

  1. Choose a Local Profile and PLAY OFFLINE on the profile screen, or select a pre-existing profile. Up to eight independent profiles are supported.
  2. Enter Mosswatch, the starting settlement in Tier 1. Learn to move with WASD or click-to-move; rotate the camera with right mouse drag.
  3. Use F5 outside combat to save an initial checkpoint. Open J to inspect Journal/Field Guide objectives.
  4. Talk to the settlement services. Learn the Outfitter, Workbench, Merchant, Sanctuary and shared stockpile rather than buying every upgrade immediately.
  5. Collect nearby wood, fibre, Flint, River Reed or other accessible regional materials. Gather by moving into range and interacting with the displayed node.
  6. Make or purchase basic equipment, equip it with E, and keep some materials for Field Dressings and early Workshop orders.
  7. Open the local route map with R, travel to a reachable encounter marker, choose Begin Encounter, target the enemy and fight. Companion assistance and dodging Guardian telegraphs matter as difficulty increases.
  8. Return to a town fire to recover, or use H for a Field Dressing when available. Save again.
  9. At the Companion Armoury, set your active/reserve team. Send an eligible reserve on the Expedition Lodge starter Bones route when practical.
  10. Begin improving Growth, gear, field skills and settlement production while clearing the campaign.

A good early loop is: explore → gather → fight → heal → equip/craft → improve companions and town → unlock the next route.

4. How the campaign and exploration work #

World uses a large ten-tier, 10,000-encounter campaign alongside physical 3D maps, towns, route markers, dungeons and landmarks. There are 1,000 sequential stages per expedition tier. Your current frontier controls encounter and equipment unlocks.

  • Tab opens the Atlas for discovered travel destinations and tiers. Town travel changes maps; it is not a seamless open-world continent.
  • R opens local route markers; selecting one causes your explorer to walk there. At the marker choose Begin Encounter.
  • Normal encounter wins advance the route. Guardians require better positioning, equipment, companion skills and attention to orange/other visible attack warnings.
  • Town landmarks, dungeon entrances and special locations reward exploration and feed discoveries into the Journal.
  • After a stage is cleared, you can use ×2, ×5 or ×10 stamina replays from that stage's route marker when available. Each repeat uses one World stamina; first-clear rewards are not paid again.
  • World stamina recovers at +1 every five minutes to a maximum of 100. This is deliberately different from Primalbound Web's stamina speed.

At the end of a tier, check its Guardian clear and tier rewards before pursuing the next tier.

5. Fighting as a human explorer #

Melee: Equip a sword, spear or other melee weapon. Click an enemy, approach into range, attack, and raise Space to guard if your equipped gear allows it. Shield stamina and attack wind-ups matter. Move clear of Guardian telegraphs; a miss or dodge can cancel damage.

Ranged: Equip a bow, crossbow or throwing weapon. Click an enemy and your character aims/releases from the appropriate range; terrain can block shots. Each released shot consumes one matching ammunition charge. The HUD distinguishes Low and No Ammo. Bows use Flight Shafts, crossbows use Latch Darts; thrown weapon families require their own charges. There is no magazine/reload mechanic in the current design. Restock via the Outfitter or Workshop.

Magic: Equip a Magic main-hand, open M to assign your four spells, and use 1–4 to cast. An offensive first-slot spell is also used for click-to-attack. Spells spend Aether, recover on cooldown, and offer attack, area, ward, heal and movement effects. Spell mastery and Magic gear affect casting; the six schools can interact with regional enemy resistance. Current Magic uses Aether, not consumable runes.

In every class, conserve healing supplies for remote routes, use physical town fires for free recovery where available, and remember that combat actions and inventory changes may be restricted until a fight ends.

6. Your dinosaurs: companions, summoning and mounts #

The Sanctuary and Companion Armoury control owned, active, reserve, away and downed companions. The active fighting party can include up to three companions, and saved party presets keep formation choices. A preview does not itself apply a party: use the explicit Apply action.

  • Press G to summon or dismiss a companion. A dismissed companion is non-colliding and does not block the Workbench or gathering nodes. A safe summon normally puts it behind/alongside you, not on top of your character.
  • V toggles Assist (helps attack your selected target) and Passive (follows without attacking). X recalls and returns to Passive.
  • Q activates the lead companion's available special when in range and with a valid target. Ember uses Pack Rally; Cinder uses Breaker Bite; Tide uses Hamstring. Their cooldowns and requirements appear in the HUD.
  • F next to a companion opens its nearby interaction menu. Attach an owned Trail Saddle and select Ride to travel faster outdoors; press F to dismount in clear ground.
  • Mounted combat, gathering, gear changes and most town interactions are unavailable; dismount first.
  • Downed companions cannot immediately be selected or sent on missions. Sanctuary or Resting Fire recovery can return a downed companion after the game's recovery timer (M22 baseline: 60 seconds to half health).
  • Your explicit summon/dismiss choice persists with newer milestones. If a summon is unsafe in the current spot, move to open ground.

Tip: When you cannot click a resource or service because your dinosaur is in the way, G dismisses it without removing ownership or changing saved companions.

7. Equipment, ammunition, loot and gear tiers #

Use E to review human weapons/armour and companion equipment. Human equipment determines whether you are currently Melee, Ranged or Magic. You can buy, craft, equip, lock and improve suitable gear at the appropriate services.

  • Early human Tier 1 gear is available at the start. Later human equipment tiers unlock through the relevant campaign tier clears; merely visiting a high-tier biome does not skip requirements.
  • Companion gear uses Fang, Carapace and Talisman-style slots and tier-clear gates. The Companion Armoury explains the required clear before a recipe can be used.
  • Items have individual ownership; equipping an item for one companion does not silently copy or take it from another.
  • Auto Equip uses eligible free gear. Locked/protected items and missing materials should not be silently consumed or stolen.
  • Use L to inspect collected or pending loot. Some rewards wait safely when the pack or gear bag is full.
  • Higher tiers, bosses and Mutant Guardians are important sources of stronger rewards. Review the in-game Quick Gear Guide rather than assuming Web drop rates apply.

8. Gathering, inventory and the shared stockpile #

World has physical gathering nodes. Approach the node and complete its interaction. Different regions offer wood, fibre, Flint, River Reed, Ash Ore, Ancient Fragments and more. Some advanced nodes require the corresponding skill level and tool.

  • Field skills include Foraging, Woodcraft, Quarrying, Fossil Excavation, Crafting and Combat progression. Profession levels cap at 50 in the M28 lineage.
  • Personal successful actions earn profession XP and mastery; worker-produced materials do not count as personal gathering actions.
  • The carried pack has a capacity measured in material units, not inventory grid cells. Merchants/outfitters offer upgrades as you progress.
  • Settlement/Storehouse storage participates in a shared stockpile. Capacity and available stock are checked before claiming supplies or creating an order; rejected transactions should not partially spend resources.
  • M28 capped offline homestead worker production at eight hours per elapsed period and at shared-stockpile capacity. No extra production is earned by moving the device clock backward.
  • Individual outdoor resource nodes regenerate after their own cooldown; depleted nodes disappear and their state survives travel or reload.

9. Homesteads, villagers, workers and defence #

World settlements are physical places rather than Web menu-only plots. B opens the homestead systems where available. You can recruit and assign residents, develop buildings and storage, maintain local workers, use caravans and specialists, and protect settlements from raids.

  • Each visited region keeps its own homes, workers, upgrades and map-local state. Workers can gather at different jobs or help with suitable specialist production.
  • Woodcraft/Foraging/Quarrying jobs, Arcanists, Guards and repair crews are separate assignments; one resident does not work every role simultaneously.
  • The shared stockpile collects ordinary worker production. Some special Magic crafting components remain tied to a local Arcanist settlement.
  • Walls, watchtowers and guards help protect developed settlements. Watch enemy telegraphs, repair damage and review stored materials before large upgrades.
  • Offline homestead output is capped. Production pauses when storage is full.

10. Workbench and crafting queue #

Walk to a physical Workbench (near an Outfitter) and open its order board.

  1. Choose a recipe and a batch that your Workbench can support.
  2. Review the exact materials/Coins before confirming. Materials are reserved at order acceptance, not at collection.
  3. Watch the active job and any waiting jobs. Jobs process in order, with speed/capacity improved by Workbench upgrades.
  4. Collect finished output from the safe completed-items tray. A full inventory does not destroy a finished item.
  5. Cancel only when you are sure; check the cancellation review for the exact returned materials.

The reviewed M27 workshop batch options were: Level 1: 1 or 3, Level 2: 1, 5 or 6, Level 3: 1, 5 or 10. Every batch item creates its own job and requires queue capacity. World Workshop production uses active-play timing; do not assume Web-style offline crafting.

M30–M30.1 Herbalist Hut (archived backfill): At the physical Mosswatch Herbalist Hut, research local plants to unlock Refined and Prime Tonics. Craft eligible tonics in batches of 1, 5 or 10 using the listed materials, including River Reed and Mosswood Fibre. Advanced tonics heal the explorer and living active companions, but cannot revive a downed companion. Facility upgrades reveal new station details without changing saved egg finish times or already accepted jobs.

11. Expedition Lodge and reserve companion missions #

Visit the physical Mosswatch Expedition Lodge. Active party members cannot be dispatched; first move an eligible companion into Reserve at the Companion Armoury.

  • Select a route, one or two available reserve companions, and confirm the time and previewed Bones/cargo rewards.
  • Fernwood Foraging is the starter route, paying 40 Bones after 15 minutes at frontier 1. Fossil Recovery follows from frontier 51, paying 120 Bones after 30 minutes.
  • Route completion can unlock more mission slots and mastery rewards; check the board for exact requirements.
  • The Journal can show mission status away from town, but Dispatch, Recall and Collect require returning to the Lodge Board.
  • Recall an unfinished mission for no reward. A ready mission should be collected rather than recalled.
  • Rewards will not silently overfill shared stock or your pack; free up room if collection is blocked.

These are timed reserve-crew missions, distinct from the player-driven Expedition Operations below.

12. Nursery, breeding and lineages #

At Mosswatch's physical Nursery, review its building level, available parents, eggs, incubation and pedigree.

  • Eligible parents are different owned dinosaurs with the same diet, at least 25% Growth, not active in your battle party, away, downed or on breeding recovery.
  • Close relatives and hybrids cannot be ordinary breeding parents.
  • The M23 base breeding cost is 1,400 Bones + 3 River Reed. Both parents then require six hours recovery.
  • A hatchling is one of its parents' species; each parent contributes a gene, and a compatible mutation can have up to 28% total inheritance chance. The child identity and outcome are fixed when the breeding action is confirmed.
  • Eggs hatch only after their displayed incubation timer. Nursery upgrades affect future eggs; existing egg completion times do not change retroactively.
  • Use the pedigree/Family Record to inspect parents, grandparents, siblings, descendants and generation. Favourite markers organise breeding stock but do not improve genetic odds.
  • Physical upgrades eventually increase egg slots and incubation efficiency; review the cost and requirements at the Nursery.

These World breeding rules are separate from Web, including its material names and exact inventory pools.

13. Growth, training, Prime and practice #

The physical Growth & Prime Focus near Mosswatch Sanctuary shows a companion's Growth, training XP, milestone benefits and awakening eligibility.

  • +1% Growth costs Bones equal to current Growth × 110 for each purchased step. +10% and Upgrade All display a full review and either commit together or not at all.
  • Growth can also advance through earned training XP from eligible fights and activities. Relevant milestones occur at 10, 20, 25, 35, 45, 50, 60, 70, 75, 85, 95 and 100%.
  • Prime Awakening unlocks at 75% Growth, costs 10 Prime Essence, and gives the individual creature +15% Attack / +20% Health. The Prime state does not change its species identity.
  • Mosswatch's Training Yard offers paid Growth drills for your current party, after reviewing the cost.
  • Training Grounds offers reached encounters, Guardians and eligible discovered Mutants in Practice/Assessment mode. No real Bones, Coins, ammo, Aether, loot, progression, DNA or permanent damage is awarded or consumed; reports describe the sample run, not a guaranteed win rate.

14. Mutant Guardians, gear sets, DNA and hybrids #

World features six special Mutant Guardian identities: Two-Headed Tyrant, Rex–Raptor Chimera, Abyss Splicer, Ironjaw Abomination, Void Stalker and Frosthowl Leviathan. Their physical lairs require the relevant progression/discovery and have visible attack warnings and phases.

  • Prepare space for the guaranteed set reward before the hunt. A successful run awards one tier-matched set piece, normal listed rewards and any eligible first-clear record.
  • First-clear DNA is associated with the Guardian and is not repeatedly farmed by replaying that same victory. Replays also do not duplicate first-clear Prime Essence.
  • Mutant set pieces have individual identity and can mix tiers for set bonuses. The four set families are Twinfang, Ironhide, Venomheart and Lifebloom; set effects operate on the World real-time combat system rather than Web turn counts.
  • Visit the physical Genetics Lab near Mosswatch Sanctuary for one of six hybrid blueprints. Review the DNA, Shards, Bones, campaign gate and resulting creature before forging. The hybrid becomes an owned creature with its own identity; ordinary Nursery breeding cannot bypass the DNA Lab.
  • Mutant sightings, victories, DNA progress and set sources are recorded in the Field Guide.

15. Field Guide, Bestiary, fossils and the Archive #

Open the Explorer Field Guide through J or its full menu. This tracks owned/missing creatures, sighting evidence, tracks, nests, battles, companion level/Growth and species background. World uses a stable mapped subset of species IDs compatible with Web where implemented, not automatic access to all Web creatures.

  • Living Bestiary specimens roam on outdoor maps. Seeing, fighting and defeating one can add research without advancing a campaign encounter.
  • M33 fossils: At named fossil sites, Fossil Excavation adds Ancient Fragments and species-linked specimens. Collection and research records persist when starting a new campaign.
  • Build the Fossil Archive in Mosswatch and study a species when you hold at least three matching specimens. Research consumes two duplicates, leaving one specimen for display; it does not directly grant combat power.
  • Open the Palaeontology Index anywhere to track samples, research, regions and trophies. Performing new studies requires returning to the physical Archive.
  • M33 3D inspection: The Field Guide roster, six hybrids and six Mutant Guardians have species-specific models where available. Optional assets may use safe registered fallback models; the saved creature's species ID stays unchanged.
  • M36.1: owned-companion Field Guide records expose current Growth, the next milestone, Prime eligibility/awakening and training progress. Lodge Rank I/II/III trophy crests appear only once the corresponding mastery is earned.

16. Regional events and active Expedition Operations #

The physical World map supports optional regional events: migrating herds, predator threats, caravan trouble, rare material blooms, fossil finds, nest/track chains, storms, rift anomalies, settlement warnings and rare Bestiary sightings. M32 introduced these regional events and active Expedition Operations. In M36.4, all ten event families are represented in the event catalog.

  • Review the event marker and its reward before resolving it. Events are deterministic, non-expiring and one-time per map, not a daily FOMO timer.
  • Wildlife-related events add appropriate sighting/track evidence to the Living Bestiary. Rewards use the existing Bones/material economy, not new event tokens, DNA or Prime Essence.
  • M36.4: Rare Nest / Track Chain, Severe Storm and Rare Bestiary Sighting complete the ten event families. Resolved event IDs persist through JSON reload; an already completed event is not a new claim after restart.
  • M32 active operations: At Mosswatch's Expedition Board, choose a visited destination, operation type, saved companion pack, and optional Field Kit. Review supplies and base/deep rewards before dispatch. The operation's seed, costs and reward snapshot are fixed at dispatch.
  • Travel to the marked location and complete three objectives. Extract for the base reward, Continue for two more objectives and the reviewed deeper reward, or Retreat for no reward.
  • Optional five-step Trailblazer challenge routes save a permanent record but do not directly pay currency.
  • The Journal's Field Events & Readiness index shows optional events, returned missions, finished orders, eggs, contract claims and Journey rewards without auto-claiming them.

17. Field Contracts and Journey Journal #

At Mosswatch, use the physical Field Contract Board for weekly activities spanning Campaign, Gathering, Crafting, Sanctuary, Exploration and Mutant Hunts. Ordinary play progresses eligible contracts automatically.

  • Review completed rewards and use Claim or Claim All at the board, where capacity rules apply. Earlier claims cannot be repeated.
  • Weekly timers use the game's protected clock, rather than letting a changed system clock repeat past rewards.
  • Journey Journal achievements span several chapters and remain earned once completed. You can pin a goal for easy reference.
  • M35 Explorer Journal: Open Creatures, Regions, Guardians, Fossils, Expeditions, Lineages, Research and Trophies from one index. Optional ready returns produce a review notice; opening the Journal never automatically claims or spends resources.

18. Settings, sound and performance #

Open Esc → Graphics & Accessibility for Low, Balanced or High graphics, frame cap, window mode, resolution, shadow/effect/model quality, HUD/text size, captions, camera shake and effect intensity. Esc → Music & Sound Settings separates Master, Music, Ambience, Effects and Interface volume/mute.

M34 audio and ambience: Eight additional offline ambience loops cover settlements and biome families (forest, wetland, coast, volcanic, ice/crystal, ruins/bones and rift). Adjust Master, Music, Ambience, Effects and Interface volume/mute independently. Existing M24 music/SFX preferences migrate without changing campaign progress. Visual Guardian telegraphs remain available when muted.

M35 graphics and performance: Use Low, Balanced or High with independent frame, resolution, shadow, model, effects and accessibility controls. These preferences are kept in a separate local settings file, not the campaign save. Raptor/tyrannosaur models can load in the background with fallbacks; large management screens and unvisited maps are deferred. Low effects trims optional visuals, not game rules.

M36.3: Melee, Ranged and Magic impacts have distinct cues, alongside shield-raise, ranged-release and spell-cast sounds. All follow the existing Effects volume/mute options. Combat sound captions can explain attack/guard/cast cues even when Effects sound is muted. Visual warning indicators remain available without sound. Preferences are saved separately from campaign progression. If the game runs slowly, try Low or Balanced, lower effects, and reduce model/shadow quality. M36.2 applies the actual Low graphics preset before loading Mosswatch, rather than only displaying Low on the settings screen.

19. Local profiles, saves and safe recovery #

M36.4 is offline-playable and does not yet offer verified live World cloud syncing. The configured preview account service had not advertised the required M36 World-save capability at the audited release. Do not assume logging into the Web game transfers a World character.

  • M36 keeps up to eight local profiles, each with its own checked campaign-save envelope and existing milestone data.
  • F5 saves locally outside restricted combat/transition states; use the pause-menu save/quit flow when ending a session.
  • From Esc → Account & Save Recovery, Export JSON Backup creates a portable backup of that profile.
  • Import as New Profile validates a backup and creates a new slot rather than overwriting another profile.
  • Local Recovery Points retains up to ten recent recovery entries. Restoring one preserves the campaign it replaces in history.
  • A damaged local save can use the last verified backup/history; if none is safe, the game should avoid silently overwriting the damaged file.
  • Older milestone imports are copies into the newer profile/save format, not an invitation to rename files between versions. Keep the original checkpoint and export a JSON before upgrading.

Local profile data is stored under the Primalbound World application-data area on Windows (typically %APPDATA%\\PrimalboundWorldPrototype), separate from the installed game folder. Updating a ZIP/launcher should not require deleting this data.

Published World milestone save compatibility (M30–M36.4)

These entries apply only to the World/Desktop Windows playtest, not Web save formats or cloud accounts. Back up the current profile as JSON before updating. Let the newer game import a copy of an older verified save; do not rename user:// files or overwrite the originals manually.

  • M30 — schema 300 (user://m30_progress.json): imports validated M29 progress into a new file; the M29 source remains unchanged. Journey records and facility progress carry forward.
  • M30.1 — schema 301 (user://m30_1_progress.json): imports valid M30 progress without editing that source file; adds plant research and tonic stock.
  • M31 — schema 310 (user://m31_progress.json): migrates valid M30.1 progress, retaining its source save; stores explorer appearance and class loadouts.
  • M32 — schema 320 (user://m32_progress.json): imports valid M31 progress without overwriting M31. Regional event claims, active Expedition Operations and Trailblazer records persist through reloads and new campaigns.
  • M33 — schema 330 (user://m33_progress.json): imports verified M32 progress, leaves M32 intact and retains palaeontology specimens and research when starting a new campaign.
  • M34 — schema 330 unchanged: adds separate audio preferences and migrates prior M24 music/SFX settings; does not introduce a new campaign save format.
  • M35 — schema 330 unchanged: Graphics & Accessibility preferences are kept separately from campaign progress. Earlier M34 audio and M33 fossil/model content remain available.
  • M36 — schema 330 in verified profile envelopes: no new campaign gameplay schema. Supports eight local profiles, import-as-new-profile from earlier valid progress, and up to ten recovery points without overwriting the source save.
  • M36.1 — schema 330 unchanged: Field Guide and Lodge presentation additions do not migrate or reset the campaign, profiles or recovery points.
  • M36.2 — schema 330 unchanged: Low graphics startup behavior changes without changing saved campaign progress, preferences or recovery data.
  • M36.3 — schema 330 unchanged: expanded combat audio keeps M35 preferences, existing profiles and recovery points; no save migration or reset.
  • M36.4 — no new campaign schema: event-claim persistence and pause-menu refinements preserve existing campaign saves and claims.

The published Windows release archive supplies the corresponding versioned ZIP, notes and checksums. None of these milestones establishes Web–World cloud or save sharing.

20. Common problems #

  • Lower pause options hidden in a small or portrait window: From M36.4, the Esc pause menu scrolls to expose lower controls. Adjust HUD/text scaling or window size if needed.
  • Launcher reaches Play but no world loads: First confirm the whole ZIP was extracted, use the latest complete launcher package, and consult PLAYTEST.txt. Export or preserve saves before changing the install.
  • Companion blocks interactions: Press G to dismiss; return to clear ground before summoning it again.
  • Q does nothing: Verify a companion is summoned, in Assist mode, with a selected valid target, in range, and its ability off cooldown.
  • Ranged cannot fire: Check the matching ammunition type, correct weapon, line of sight and target range. There is no reload button.
  • Magic spell unavailable: Check the equipped Magic main-hand, the Spellbook's four slots, current Aether, unlocks and cooldown.
  • Cannot collect a mission or Workshop order: Check that the correct physical station is in range and you have enough inventory/shared storage capacity.
  • Cannot breed: Check same-diet parents, Growth, family relationship, reserve status, Recovery and egg slots.
  • No cloud option: Expected for the current World playtest; continue using local profiles and JSON exports.
  • Can't equip higher-tier gear: Verify the exact required campaign-tier final clear and return to the correct physical service.
  • An update appears but you want to keep playing: Use PLAY CURRENT VERSION. Updating is optional.

21. Features not included in M36.4 #

The following should not be advertised as already playable: shared Web–Desktop accounts/progression or automatic cross-save conversion; server-authoritative World multiplayer/co-op; final Steam distribution; and the proposed paid Auto Rounds / automatic encounter-heal-next routine. Those belong to later design/implementation work, not the current M36.4 public player guide.
