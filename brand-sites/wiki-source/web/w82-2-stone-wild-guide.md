# Stone & Wild: Fieldwork, Gear & Habitats — W82.2 guide

## Newer Web Preview features: W82.2 — Stone & Wild: Fieldwork, Gear & Habitats

**Publication status:** Verified live at the official **primalboundgame.com/web/** game address on **19 November 2026 (AEST)**, at Web build **43dbf0821227a841b1557b588f156f37daff1a5c**. The five reviewed release notes match this interface pass. W82 remains the numbered gameplay milestone, W83 is next, and Web save format 34 remains unchanged.

W82.2 brings the forest-and-parchment interface into Operations planning, the Armoury, Journey milestones and rules, and Sanctuary Habitats. These screens use your existing companions, equipment, goals and activity records. The numbered gameplay roadmap remains at **W82**, with **W83 event administration next**. This interface pass does not schedule or launch Season Zero. **Web save format 34 remains unchanged**, and World/Desktop has separate saves and updates.

### W82.2 finding the new screens

The quick navigation and **All activities** drawer keep their existing routes. On desktop, **Operations** opens **Expedition Operations**, and **Journey** opens **Expedition Journal**. On phones, use the available quick routes or **Open navigation** to reach the full activity list.

| Screen | Where to open it | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Operations planning | Expedition Operations → Operations | Numbered route, crew and dispatch-review sections |
| Armoury | All activities → Armoury | Individual companion dossier, inventory browser and item comparison |
| Journey milestones | Expedition Journal → Journey milestones | Earned-goal summary, current goal, chapter selection and reward cards |
| Journey automation | Expedition → Journey settings | Profile preview, live rules, Offline Journey and saved setup |
| Habitats | Sanctuary → Habitats | Habitat cards, resident portraits, search and decoration progress |

### W82.2 planning Operations and bringing cargo home

Open **Expedition Operations** and choose **Operations**. On a phone, use the **Operations section** selector. All eight existing workspaces remain available: **Discoveries & Relics**, **Regional Outlooks**, **Ancient Hunts**, **Operations**, **Challenge Board**, **Fossil Archive**, **Outpost Research** and **Field log**.

1. **Choose the route.** Select the operation type, an available region, its route condition and field supplies. Frontier progress, research and event availability still determine which choices can be used. Current conditions and any targeted fossil recovery remain part of the plan.
2. **Choose your crew.** Search the illustrated companion list or filter to **On crew**. Select one to three owned companions, then use the movement controls to arrange **Frontline**, **Centre** and **Rear**. The crew count and page controls stay visible. Browsing or searching does not change the battle party.
3. **Review your expedition.** Read the destination, route length, exact Herbs/Stone cost, available supplies, crew order, rare-specimen chance and per-node reward preview. If the plan is blocked, read the reason before dispatching. Choose **DISPATCH CREW** only when the plan is ready.

The active-operation view shows the **Dispatch formation**, the recorded companion portraits and the complete expedition trail. Nodes distinguish cleared encounters, the next encounter, a fight already in progress and uncleared encounters. Crew, formation, equipment and route conditions retain the original dispatch snapshot.

The **UNCLAIMED FIELD CARGO** panel shows the Bones and fossil finds being carried. Clearing a node does not immediately put that cargo into your inventory. Between eligible nodes, choose **EXTRACT CARGO** to bring it home; **RETREAT · NO CARGO** ends an empty active route. The usual battle locks still apply. If an operation is interrupted, its interrupted encounter cannot be replayed and already-cleared cargo remains available to extract. **Field log** retains the recent expedition records.

### W82.2 managing the Armoury

Open **All activities → Armoury** and look for the **EQUIPMENT & INVENTORY** page heading. Its material totals and capacity summary reflect your save. Five workspaces remain available:

| Workspace | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Loadout & Inventory | Inspect an individual companion, browse items and compare equipment |
| Set Field Guide | Review set bonuses, encounter sources and targeted set loot |
| Saved equipment builds | Keep named exact-item builds and review their application |
| Gear Forge | Review the existing upgrade, duplicate and reforge actions |
| Bulk breakdown | Review unwanted equipment and recover its stated materials |

In **Loadout & Inventory**, select a **Loadout group** and then a companion portrait. The dossier shows that companion's name, species, level, Growth, Prime state, **Individual record**, equipped-slot count and equipment Attack/Health contribution. Companions with matching names or species remain separate individuals. The displayed Armoury pack is a browsing choice; choosing it does not itself switch the battle party.

Use **Search other dinosaurs** to inspect a reserve outside the displayed pack. The selected companion remains the target of equipment actions. **AUTO EQUIP SELECTED** and **AUTO EQUIP DISPLAYED PACK** retain their existing rules and do not take gear from another dinosaur or break active set bonuses.

Use **Browse inventory**, the slot and set filters, equipment search, sorting and page controls to find an item. Select it, then open **Item comparison** to review that exact item's stats, current owner and effect on the selected companion. The **Companion loadout**, **Browse inventory** and **Item comparison** links move between the three sections on a small screen.

Equipping, upgrading, protecting, replacing and breaking down equipment keep their existing costs and checks. A replacement that loses an active set bonus still requires its replacement confirmation. **PROTECT ITEM** prevents breakdown, and an equipped item must be unequipped before it can be broken down. Read the exact Bones/Stone cost and result before confirming a forge or breakdown action. Merely inspecting a portrait, selecting an item or changing a workspace spends nothing.

### W82.2 following Journey milestones

Open **Expedition Journal** and select **Journey milestones**. The existing **Journal section** selector also reaches this view. The journal contains **45 permanent goals across ten chapters**. Its summary separates **Rewards ready**, **In progress** and **Rewards claimed**; earned progress and claimed rewards remain distinct.

The **Current Journey goal** panel shows your pinned goal, a ready reward or the next suggested milestone. Choose **Show this milestone** to reveal its chapter, clear the other filters, bring its card into view and focus that card. Pinning another eligible goal changes what you follow; it does not pay a reward.

Choose a chapter in the desktop list or use **Journey chapter** on a phone. Each chapter shows its real earned and ready counts. Combine chapter selection with **All**, **Ready**, **In progress** or **Claimed**, or search by milestone name and description. **Clear milestone filters** returns to the full journal. Claimed cards retain their recorded reward amounts.

Each goal card shows the requirement, current progress, exact reward, pin state and **Go to activity** action. Claim one ready reward from its card, or use **Claim ready journey rewards** to collect the displayed total for all ready goals. Rewards are still paid once per save. Weekly contracts, daily contracts, expedition caches and Season Zero rewards remain separate; no new rewards are granted simply by opening the redesigned journal.

### W82.2 setting Journey rules and preparing an offline run

Open **Expedition → Journey settings**. **Auto Journey 2.0** now groups the controls around your current rules, target stage and Offline Journey state.

Select one of the six existing safety profiles to preview its real mode, Health stop, stamina reserve, storage threshold, fight limit and power margin. The selected preview is separate from **Current rules**. **Apply Journey profile** applies those safety rules; it does not start a fight, enable Auto Advance, change the target stage, change the pack, equip gear or spend Bones.

**Live Journey rules** has two sections. **Progression** contains Auto Advance, advancement mode, Elite/boss and tier stops, target stage and maximum fights. **Pack and supplies** contains power margin, remaining Health, stamina reserve and storage conditions. Each control retains its help text and existing meaning, including when zero disables an optional limit. These rules govern campaign frontier progression; repeat farming and Rift runs use their own controls.

In **Offline Journey**, enable Auto Advance before preparing your current frontier, pack and rules. Once prepared, the rules are locked until you resolve or cancel the run. The displayed allowance grows by one fight per elapsed minute, up to 20; your target, stop rules and battle results can reduce the number actually completed. Use **Resolve offline fights** when eligible, or **Cancel offline Journey** to release the prepared setup. Do not change the pack or equipment while a run is prepared.

**Saved Journey setup** shows whether a loadout exists and how many companions its saved formation contains. **Save Journey loadout** retains the formation and rules; **Apply Journey loadout** uses the existing availability and battle checks. It does not restore Auto Equip or paid Growth settings. **Optional equipment and Growth automation** remains a separate expandable section. Those existing options can equip items or spend Bones after eligible frontier victories, so review their help text and Bones reserve before enabling them.

### W82.2 arranging Sanctuary Habitats

Open **Sanctuary → Habitats** and choose **Unassigned**, **Forest**, **Wetlands**, **Plains**, **Desert** or **Alpine**. Group counts show currently available reserves. Active, deployed, travelling or retired companions do not appear as available residents, but their saved home assignments remain recorded.

The selected habitat shows its field notes, named decoration and progress toward unlocking it. **Find a resident** searches the companion name, species or individual record. The resident browser displays **24 matching companions per page**, with **Previous residents** and **Next residents** controls. Family groups remain together in the presentation where possible; larger groups can continue on another page. Favourites are prioritised within the family grouping.

Each portrait card shows the named companion, species, relevant Prime/Hybrid/generation labels and its individual record. Use **Habitat for [name]** to move that specific available reserve. The assignment control applies the change directly and reports whether it succeeded. Same-name companions retain separate assignments. Bred companions keep their **View family** action. Search and paging do not move any companion.

Assigning three available reserves to a habitat permanently unlocks its existing decoration: **Fern Arch**, **Reed Fountain**, **Sunstone Circle**, **Sandstone Totem** or **Frost Pine**. The decoration remains unlocked when residents leave. A temporarily unavailable companion keeps its home and returns to that habitat's available-resident list when eligible again. The interface does not create extra residents, spend resources on an assignment or change habitat upgrade rules.

### W82.2 progress and artwork

This pass reuses the existing approved creature portraits and existing fallbacks. It does not add new species, new hybrid blueprints, new rewards or a season launch. Existing named companions, individual IDs, equipment, cargo, goals, family records, decorations, accounts and cloud saves continue using the same gameplay rules and **Web format 34**. W83 remains the next numbered feature milestone.
