# Stone & Wild: Complete Creature Artwork — W82.3 guide

## Newer Web Preview features: W82.3 — Stone & Wild: Complete Creature Artwork

**W82.3 — 19 November 2026 (AEST).** The 172-creature gallery is complete. Open Sanctuary or the Codex to see the pictures, with matching portraits in the Armoury, fieldwork, Habitats, the DNA Lab and battles.

W82.3 completes individual portrait coverage for all **172 catalog creatures**. It adds **119 new full-body paintings** and retains the **53 existing individual portraits**. The new pictures follow the Stone & Wild painted style and use transparent backgrounds so their full silhouettes fit the game's companion cards, records and activity views.

### W82.3 what received new artwork

| Previous artwork | New portraits | What changed |
|---|---:|---|
| No image source; a glyph or silhouette fallback | 97 | Individual paintings for the remaining Fliers, Oceans, Mammals, Ancient Wildlife, Deepwilds and Mythic Beasts |
| Basic field-guide SVG drawing | 20 | Detailed paintings for creatures including Suchomimus, Deinocheirus, Dodo, Titanoboa and Sarcosuchus |
| Hybrid assembled from parent portraits | 2 | Dedicated full-creature paintings for Nightfall and Frostbane |
| Existing individual portrait | 53 retained | The previous 7 Stone & Wild portraits and 46 other individual pictures remain part of the complete gallery |

The total is **119 new portraits + 53 retained portraits = 172 catalog identities**. This is the artwork catalog count, not a limit on how many individual companions you can own. The update adds pictures to existing creatures; it does not add new species, hybrid blueprints or recruitment requirements.

### W82.3 where to see the portraits

| Screen | Where the artwork appears |
|---|---|
| Sanctuary | Companion cards and the record for the selected individual |
| Codex | The creature's catalog entry and portrait |
| Armoury | The selected companion's dossier and equipment context |
| Fieldwork and Operations | Crew choices, recorded crew and the associated creature views |
| Sanctuary → Habitats | Resident cards, including renamed companions and bred family members |
| DNA Lab and battles | The correct catalog creature or hybrid portrait |

The same catalog identity supplies the picture across these screens. A creature that previously had a silhouette, a simple drawing or a parent-image composite now uses its dedicated painting wherever that identity is shown. The existing activity routes and controls remain in place.

### W82.3 Nightfall and Frostbane

**Nightfall — Void Stalker** now has one integrated portrait based on its existing **Giganotosaurus + Troodon** lineage. **Frostbane — Frosthowl Leviathan** has one integrated portrait based on **Yutyrannus + Tyrannosaurus**. Frostbane's Leviathan name does not turn it into an ocean creature: its existing lineage is terrestrial.

Both pictures appear consistently in companion records, the DNA Lab and the matching battles. Their existing names, abilities, unlocks and gameplay rules are unchanged. The older W82.1 chapter describes the composite artwork used at that release; this chapter records the new dedicated paintings.

### W82.3 renamed and bred companions

A portrait follows the companion's species or hybrid identity. Renaming a companion does not remove its picture or substitute another creature. Bred companions retain the appropriate artwork while keeping their own name, individual record, generation and family information.

Two companions with the same name or species may display the same species painting. They remain **separate individuals** with their own equipment, progress, assignments and family records. Use the selected companion's name and **Individual record** when choosing equipment, crew or a habitat resident. The picture is a visual reference; it does not merge companion records or change which individual an action targets.

### W82.3 progress and save compatibility

This artwork release keeps **Web save format 34** and the existing gameplay rules. It does not change combat statistics, abilities, loot, rewards, recruitment, equipment, habitat costs or progression. Existing accounts, cloud saves, named companions, held cargo, family records and decorations continue as before.

The numbered gameplay roadmap remains at **W82**, with **W83 event administration next**. Season Zero remains unscheduled. World/Desktop remains a separate game with separate saves and release notes.

Reopen or refresh the game at **primalboundgame.com/web/** to load the current artwork. There is no need to rename a companion, breed it again or create a new save to receive a portrait. If an image still fails to load, note the creature, screen and displayed game build when reporting it; the existing identity labels and controls remain available.
