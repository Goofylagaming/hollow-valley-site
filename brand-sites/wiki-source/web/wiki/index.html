W40.5 — Keep Device & Account Campaigns Separate #

Verified LIVE Web-only update. Render deployed W40.5 gameplay notes at commit WIKITOKEN0END on 30 October 2026 at 12:27 AEST. The successful build ran 823 tests, generated build-manifest.json and attached the exact-commit player notes with gameplay manifest SHA-256 dd1d5dec0fe90b807e467811a4c04c0c0311f308a94e113a05a5b89c85dfe11e. W40.5 is an account/device campaign recovery fix, not new battle content.

  1. Recognise your two Web campaigns. Your browser's Device Save is separate from the last campaign loaded for a signed-in Player Account. A lost or expired account session must not copy that account campaign over the original device campaign. Progress for different accounts remains separate rather than being automatically merged.
  2. Use an offline account copy deliberately. When the Player Account sign-in panel offers Load Local Account Copy, check the displayed Stage and Bones, then select it only if you want that saved account campaign on this device. This does not sign in, change the cloud, upload progress or merge campaigns. Your current device campaign is kept as a recovery copy.
  3. Change accounts or load a cloud campaign safely. Compare device and account progress before choosing the replacement. The game retains local account and device recovery copies before a cloud switch, a manual import or a previous-save restoration. A failed validation or insufficient browser storage blocks replacement; use Legacy & Saves to download JSON backups and resolve the warning instead of forcing the operation.
  4. Cancel safely; sign out only with a valid device save. Cancelling a local recovery leaves both stored campaigns untouched. On sign-out, the latest separate device save is checked again; unreadable or newer data will prevent an unsafe switch and prompt you to review/reload it. Signing out never merges account progress into the device campaign.
  5. Keep independent backup copies. Export the current Device Save and any confirmed Cloud History revision before experimenting. Cloud revisions, an account's local recovery copy and the independent device campaign are different safeguards and should not be treated as interchangeable.

Save compatibility: W40.5 keeps Web format 23. Existing companions, Bones, currencies, rewards, missions, progression and account ownership are unchanged; no extra rewards or retroactive campaign migration are implied. Do not load format-23 Web saves in older Web builds or import them into World/Desktop (which uses its own local profile schema).

Earn regional standing and optional research #

  1. Open Operations → Regional Outlooks → Regional research for the selected one of 20 Web regions. Earn standing by fully clearing and extracting an eligible route: a full Survey or Recover return grants 2 standing, while another completed ordinary route grants 1. Partial returns, failed routes and Challenge returns give no standing. Existing work is not back-credited.
  2. Progress through Field Scout, Regional Surveyor and Trailkeeper using the region's actual standing, full Survey and Recover evidence, and (where required) full returns under all four weather outlooks. Rank and evidence belong to the specific region, not your whole account.
  3. After 6 standing plus both a full Survey and Recover, spend 5 Research Insight to unlock that region's Field Supply Permit. For future weather expeditions only, it removes one additional weather supply: Herbs first, otherwise Stone. It does not remove the ordinary expedition kit.
  4. After 12 standing, full Survey and Recover evidence and a complete return under all four outlooks, spend 8 Insight on Specimen Index. It adds 2 percentage points to the region's rare specimen chance while respecting the existing 42% final cap. Read the final quote before dispatch; purchase never changes an already-frozen route or held job.

Plan persistent Field opportunities #

  1. In Operations → Regional Outlooks → Field opportunities, first earn Field Scout standing for a region. Browse its current opportunity and three future opportunities. They are persistent: no expiry timer, time window or daily reset rerolls them.
  2. Pick Exposed Fossil Beds, Herb Blooms, Migrating Herds or Predator Pressure when offered. Each is a fixed three-node route with a disclosed threat, Bones/supplies and potential rewards. It replaces the normal special condition; it cannot stack with Weather Outlooks or Challenges. Migrating Herds follow a known regional creature through the encounters and fossil finds, while Predator Pressure ends with a creature already available to the region. Eligible enemies may expand as you progress normally.
  3. Check the full-route-only Herbs or Stone separately from already-cleared base cargo. Dispatch freezes the crew, chosen opportunity, enemies, supplies and reward terms. Clear all three nodes and extract to obtain the completion materials and advance the opportunity. A nonempty partial extraction keeps only earned base cargo; failure, retreat and simply letting time pass never reroll an opportunity.
  4. Collect the return safely. If the cargo, wallet, opportunity state or any other capacity/validation check fails, the entire return (including standing, research-related state and completion materials) stays pending. Do not attempt to bypass a held-return warning. Original standard, weather and Challenge route generation remains available and unchanged. The Compsognathus portrait/fossil identity is used for Compy within the new opportunities.

Save and apply named equipment loadouts #

  1. Open Equipment loadouts for an intended crew of one to three owned companions. Save up to five named loadouts recording the exact owned gear items and their current upgrades. These are equipment sets, distinct from W38's per-companion Talent Builds and the saved Frontline/Centre/Rear pack formations.
  2. Before applying, review the complete item moves, Attack, Health and equipment set-effect changes. Empty recorded slots mean an item will be unequipped, not replaced automatically. A valid reviewed loadout moves everything together among that saved crew without changing the active party or formation.
  3. If an item has been sold, salvaged or is worn by a companion outside the recorded crew, or if a required companion is unavailable, the whole application is blocked. Update the loadout rather than retrying a stale preview. Saving, replacing or forgetting a preset never moves or destroys gear. Backups preserve the saved preset definitions.

Targeted Fossil Archive recovery #

  1. From the Fossil Archive, search or filter the 166 eligible natural species and use the species-specific targeted Recover planning route. Biology I and the species' cleared discovery stage are required. This is not a new hybrid, DNA or exclusive-equipment farming method.
  2. Review the route's normal kit plus 1 Stone and its 10% reduction to Bones earnings, then dispatch. The three fossil finds are targeted to the species, but duplicates are still possible. Dispatch locks target and route terms; only genuinely cleared and extracted cargo may be collected.
  3. Targeted recovery provides a legitimate source for Pale Thunderer's 45 and Riftback Sovereign's 60 distinct-fragment Ancient Hunt prerequisites. All their other stage, research, Trail and weakness-analysis gates still apply. The Fossil Archive and companion-history views use compact records and lazy-loaded goals; the Outpost still shows held crafting-order status.

W38.0 — Living Regions: Regional Outlooks #

Open Operations → Regional outlook to review conditions for any of the 20 Web regions. This is an optional kind of Expedition Operation: the old standard conditions and Challenge routes still exist. The outlook board shows the active named forecast plus the next three regional returns. A locked region's forecast may be inspected, but you must meet its stage gate before dispatch.

  1. Choose the region and its current forecast. Pick Regional outlook while planning an Operation rather than the normal route modifier. Regional outlooks do not stack with Challenge Expeditions or Prime Territory; their planner options remain separate.
  2. Review the exact trade-offs before dispatch. Fair: normal threat/Bones, no extra supplies. Wet: +8% threat, −8% Bones, +10 percentage points rare specimens, 1 Herb. Dry: +10% threat, +8% Bones, −4 rare points, 1 Stone. Rough: +18% threat, +12% Bones, +4 rare points, 1 Herb and 1 Stone. The final rare chance still caps at 42%; route, kit and research effects are included in the actual preview. Trust the current quoted cost and capacity checks.
  3. Dispatch only after reviewing the crew and supplies. The app rechecks the current quote and crew. Confirming locks the chosen region, outlook, encounter threat, supplies, crew, enemies and cargo into the Operation. A changed quote or stale battle result must be reviewed again; editing the normal pack cannot alter an already-dispatched run.
  4. Clear at least one node and extract to advance that region's forecast by one step. A full route can award its usual completed-route honours. A partial, non-empty extraction advances weather but does not count as a full Survey/route honour; an empty retreat does not advance it. Only that region moves. Normal Operations, elapsed time, being offline and logging back in do not move any outlook.
  5. Collect held returns normally. Rewards and weather advancement use the saved extraction record together. If currency, cargo or validation blocks collection, the entire return stays held; duplicate extraction does not grant rewards or advance a forecast twice. Active operations show their frozen outlook even if the board has moved afterward.

Save compatibility: W38.0 uses Web save format 18. Previous format-16 campaigns pass through the new format-17 companion/talent metadata without fabricating individual achievements, then initialise format-18 regional outlook counters without inventing past regional visits. Existing companions, inventory, currencies, active frozen Operations, missions, Ancient Hunts, dungeons, rewards and timers remain intact. Back up a Device Save and a verified Cloud History revision before updating. Do not open a format-18 save in an older Web client; World/Desktop local saves cannot be imported. Open its individual profile; set its personal name, inspect its species, Growth/Prime state, family and favourite-breeder link, choose an earned cosmetic title and browse credited field records. The eleven honours record eligible new actions from their actual crew, even when members were downed. Review before applying, then save up to three named exact builds per companion. Saving/removing preset names does not spend anything. Browse/search 91 possible exhibits across eight categories, including campaign banners, dungeons, Titans, Ancient Hunts, Prime Trials, Frontier, Challenges and companion honours. Filter by earned status (12 exhibits per page), then choose and arrange up to six actually earned trophies for a cosmetic showcase. Held rewards do not unlock an exhibit before collection; older dates and individual participation are never guessed. Saved showcase IDs give no bonus stats, items, currency or XP. Exhibit links lead to the corresponding existing activity or owned companion record.

W36.0 — Unified Expedition Journal #

Open the Expedition Journal from the Outpost or the Journal navigation tab. W36.0 turns the old Journey Journal into one read-only directory of existing discoveries, activities and records. It does not replace the separate combat, Codex, Workshop, Hatchery, Sanctuary, Operations or research screens, and it does not automatically claim anything. The original 30 Journey milestones still work inside the Journal (choose Journey milestones from the Journal or its Outpost shortcut); keep using their existing chapter progress, pin and Claim controls.

  1. Choose one of nine record sections: Creatures, Regions, Guardians, Fossils, Expeditions, Ancient Hunts, Lineages, Research and Trophies. Creatures search across the existing 172-species catalog and owned companion names; Regions show the current tier and previously completed regional counts. Fossils list genuine owned specimen pieces alongside species names and familiar aliases.
  2. Search and narrow the records: search within a section, use its available status filter, and, for Guardians, choose a campaign-tier filter. Each result page shows 24 records. Guardian browsing initially follows your current tier. Your selected section, search and filters remain selected when you open a linked activity and return during the same visit; browser reload may reset these preferences.
  3. Open a record to go to its existing activity: Creature entries open the relevant Codex record, Fossils open the matching Archive set, Lineages focus recorded family identities, Research focuses an existing project, and Ancient Hunts jump to the matching preparation/attempt. Guardian entries navigate only to an encounter preview: a locked Guardian remains locked and your campaign stage/frontier does not advance.
  4. Check waiting work in Expeditions: the Journal's ready/active records can point to returned Sanctuary Mission teams, hatch-ready eggs, finished Workshop orders, field operations/dungeon cargo and Ancient Hunt rewards. Go to the activity screen and deliberately collect there; opening an Expedition Journal entry never spends resources, completes an operation or grants cargo. A currently active Hunt's held reward is prioritized over its catalog card.
  5. Keep records distinct: the Trophies view uses already collected Ancient, Prime, Frontier and Challenge awards (not speculative future victories); Lineages keeps separate dinosaurs with their own individual and parent identities. Existing Guardian, fossil and family data are indexed from your actual save, not invented by navigation.

World/Desktop saves are separate and cannot be imported automatically.

W35.0 — Ancient Hunts & Relic Blueprints #

Ancient Hunts are five permanent, natural-Ancient boss fights inside Web Operations. They are not Mutant Guardians, campaign battles, passive Sanctuary Missions, Ancient Dungeon routes or the World/Desktop expedition system. Use Operations → Ancient Hunts after preparing research and region evidence; reaching a campaign stage alone does not unlock a Hunt.

  1. Earn real region evidence: finish and extract a Survey or Recover Operation in the Hunt's region. Reach the Ancient Studies rank, collect the required number of unique fossil fragments, and finish/extract the corresponding five-node Ancient Trail. A partial run, abandoned mission or an old random encounter name is not an unlock. Previously dispatched Operations keep their original saved encounters and payouts.
  2. Clear the campaign gate and analyse the weakness: once all proofs are satisfied, spend the listed Research Insight once to unlock that Hunt. Inspect the proof checklist before spending. Repeated attempts do not repeat the analysis charge.
  3. Review the actual crew: select available companions in Frontline / Centre / Rear, check their equipped gear, Growth and combat stats, and inspect the stamina and reward preview. Companions away on Sanctuary Missions cannot dispatch or begin a Hunt. Confirm entry to pay stamina once and freeze the Hunt's crew, equipment, enemy and rewards; press the separate Begin battle action when ready. A stale or invalid review cannot enter.
  4. Respond to the signature attacks: the Colossus uses root cover and a flank opening; Ashmaw's eruption leads to an exhausted damage window; the Drowned Monarch alternates land/submerged phases and targets vulnerable survivors; Pale Thunderer's charge can be intercepted; Riftback Sovereign's pulse/disorientation can be mitigated by keeping a living Centre Support, Scout or Defender, or the Centre-anchoring Riftback Compass. Ordinary individual-Health combat rules, abilities, equipment and formation still matter.
  5. Resume and claim carefully: a prepared Hunt may resume after reload, but reloading during an active fight interrupts that attempt without rewards or a stamina refund. Defeat/abandonment does not delete owned companions or equipment. Victory places rewards in a pending-collection state until you collect safely; an unsafe Bones/Prime Essence total or a full Insight bank blocks all cargo rather than granting some of it. Free capacity first, then retry collection.

W34.0 — Dungeon Practice & Pack Comparisons #

Dungeon Practice rehearses all 30 five-floor Ancient Dungeon routes without paying stamina or starting a real run. Open Training Grounds → Dungeon Runs, or use Practice on any dungeon card. A locked route can be previewed here, but practising it does not unlock the route or bypass its real campaign gate.

  1. Select a dungeon, then compare the current Frontline / Centre / Rear formation with up to five saved pack presets (six packs total). Each uses its own current members, equipped gear, Growth, genes, talents, Prime and other live combat bonuses. Fix any missing, unowned, duplicate or invalid-stat companion before rerunning a rejected pack.
  2. Press Run Practice. The simulator performs 20 deterministic complete-route samples per pack, with identical route/sample/floor seed schedules for fair comparisons. Each starts fresh at floor 1 with full Health and its present loadout; it is not a resume of an active paid dungeon run. The ordinary 120-round cap per battle also applies here; hitting it produces an unresolved sample, not a victory.
  3. Compare full clears, defeats, unresolved round limits, average floors cleared, most common stopping floor, and per-floor reach/clear counts. For any floor, Health-before-rest and recovered HP averages include only samples that reached it. Full-clear totals are practice samples, not a calibrated prediction of your win probability.
  4. Inspect the first sample's replay to review its floor progression, individual Health, targeting and Guardian/mutant warnings. Try a different party order, gear or companion loadout and rerun; changes to formation or Sanctuary Mission assignments invalidate old reports so stale results do not appear current.
  5. Companions away on Sanctuary Missions may be rehearsed without recalling them. Their practice report will warn you that they must return before a real dungeon entry. You may also rehearse while a real dungeon run is frozen; the simulation never resumes, spends resources from or changes that run.

Practice isolation and saves: This is free, rewardless simulation. It consumes no stamina, awards no Bones, Essence, Shards, gear, XP, DNA, contracts, Journey progress or dungeon records, and does not move equipment or affect pending real-run cargo. Real five-floor carryover rules apply inside each simulated sample: survivor Health carries forward and recovers 20% of maximum after non-final clears; downed companions stay down. W34.0 keeps Web save format 15; existing campaigns, dungeon checkpoints, cloud/device history and previously earned rewards remain unchanged. Export a current Device Save and confirmed Cloud History revision before changing game versions, and never downgrade a format-15 Web save to an older Web client. World/Desktop saves and Training systems are separate.

W33.0 — Playable Dungeon Expeditions #

Ancient Dungeons are now playable five-floor battles, not instant pack-power checks. All 30 existing routes retain their stage gates and route IDs. At W33.0 launch their entry costs were 5–25 stamina; W34.1 has superseded those prices with 3–9 stamina for the original six and 9–13 for later routes. Use the live route-card price. Open Ancient Dungeons, select an unlocked route, then review your ordered Frontline / Centre / Rear crew, equipment, reward preview and available stamina. Companions away on Sanctuary Missions cannot enter the run.

  1. Press the route's Enter/Begin action to pay the displayed stamina once. Entry freezes your crew, equipment, camp combat bonuses, current ascension, enemy floors, currencies and one possible gear reward. Changing your live pack afterward does not change this run.
  2. Start Floor 1, fight in the normal turn-based combat screen, and complete each floor in sequence. Regular abilities, role/formation synergies, equipped set effects and Guardian warnings now matter. Floor 3 is an elite sentinel; Floor 5 is the final Guardian. The Veilstalker Warrens finale uses Veilstalker's camouflage behaviour.
  3. Health persists between floors. After clearing a non-final floor, surviving companions regain 20% of their maximum Health, rounded up and capped at full; downed companions do not revive. Review the survivors and choose when to start the next floor. A companion assigned to a Sanctuary Mission while the run is pending must return before you can continue.
  4. Between cleared floors, you can leave the screen or browser and resume the stored checkpoint. If the browser reloads during an active battle, the fight becomes interrupted: you cannot retry that same floor within that run, and must retreat for consolation Bones. No timer silently advances a floor.
  5. After a full clear, collect your fixed rewards once: the route's existing Bones, Prime Essence, Mutation Shards, any eligible first-clear Shards, and a gear roll. Tier 2–10 dungeons award one non-set Legendary item from their own tier, subject to your cleared campaign stage at entry; Tier 1 uses its existing gear pool. Collection is atomic: if inventory space, a duplicate item ID or an unsafe wallet would prevent all rewards, cargo stays pending until collection can safely succeed.

1. The short version — what should a new player do? #

If you are brand new:

  1. Keep a full active pack of up to three dinosaurs.
  2. Push campaign stages until you start losing.
  3. When stuck, improve your pack in this order:
    • Growth
    • Talents
    • Equipment
    • Equipment levels
    • Mutations
    • Prime
    • Better species / better team synergy
  4. Gather Bones, Herbs and Stone from the Outpost while you are away.
  5. Craft useful equipment and camp upgrades in the Workshop.
  6. Repeat cleared bosses or dungeons for gear.
  7. From Expedition Tier 2 (stage 1,001+), farm Mutant Guardians for full equipment sets and first-clear DNA.
  8. Send reserve dinosaurs on Sanctuary Missions so your inactive collection keeps earning supplies.
  9. Use Training Grounds before spending resources if you want to compare packs against a difficult stage.
  10. Claim your Daily Login Reward, and check Daily Hunting Contracts, Weekly Field Contracts and Journey rewards regularly.

The main progression loop is:

Campaign → Bones/Prime/Shards/Gear → improve dinosaurs → beat harder Guardians → unlock better systems → repeat.

6. Guardian mechanics #

Every 10th campaign level is a Guardian.

Current Guardian rules include:

  • A Guardian Sweep every fourth round.
  • A warning appears one round before the Sweep.
  • Guardians enter Enraged state at 40% Health or lower.
  • Stuns can prevent some Guardian actions.
  • Higher-tier normal Guardians are deliberately stronger than surrounding normal encounters.
  • Final Guardians are stronger again and pay better rewards.

Use the Training Grounds if you are unsure whether your current pack can handle a Guardian.

  2. Enter to spend the route's stamina once and lock the current team, equipment, combat bonuses, five floors and reward roll. You can change your outside party later without changing the frozen expedition.

On defeat or retreat, only floor-proportional consolation Bones are awarded: floor((full Bones / 2) × cleared floors / 5). You receive no Essence, Shards, gear or stamina refund.

36. Training Grounds #

W34.0 divides Training Grounds into Campaign & Prime Trials and Dungeon Runs. All practice is free: no stamina, currency, XP, loot, DNA, contracts, dungeon records or campaign progression is earned, and real encounters, saved equipment and saved dungeon runs are untouched.

Campaign & Prime Trials #

Use Campaign & Prime Trials to test a current or saved battle pack against stages 1–10,000, ordinary fights, Guardians and Mutants, plus optional Prime Trial previews (introduced with W27.1). Each report runs 20 deterministic samples and summarises wins/losses, damage, healing, survivor Health, winning rounds and a replay. Practising a Prime Trial does not claim rank rewards or unlock a mastery.

Dungeon Runs — W34.0 #

  1. In Training Grounds, switch to Dungeon Runs; alternatively select Practice on a dungeon card. All 30 routes are available for preview, even when the real route is locked. Routes are grouped by Expedition Tier.
  2. Select the current Frontline / Centre / Rear formation and as many as five saved presets. The simulation uses each pack's currently equipped gear, Growth, genes, talents, Prime and global combat bonuses; it does not strip or move items.
  3. Start the run. For each pack, 20 seeded full-route samples attempt floors 1–5. Each starts fresh at full Health. Floor 3 remains elite; floor 5 remains Guardian. Survivor Health carries over and recovers 20% maximum between non-final floors; downed members stay down. A 120-round cap per fight marks a stalled sample unresolved rather than a win.
  4. Compare full clears, defeats, unresolved samples, average floors and stopping floor, floor-by-floor reach and clear counts, Health before rest and actual recovered HP. The first sample has a floor-by-floor round replay, including targeting and warnings. These samples compare loadouts; do not mistake them for calibrated real-world win odds.
  5. Changing formation or Sanctuary Mission assignments invalidates affected reports; run practice again after modifying your team. Away companions may be rehearsed without recall, but are ineligible for actual paid dungeon entry until home. A real saved dungeon checkpoint stays untouched.

Compatibility: Dungeon Practice requires no new save fields. W34.0 remains Web format 15, with no reset or migration from W33.0. This practice simulator is unrelated to the World/Desktop 3D Training Yard.

37. Saves, cloud accounts and recovery (W40.5) #

Primalbound Web can keep a Device Save in this browser and, where account services are available, a separate Cloud Save. Cloud account login uses a username/password separate from Hollow Valley or Steam accounts. These saves do not merge automatically. Desktop/World has a separate save system and does not automatically share these characters or resources.

Current save safety — W40.5 #

The latest verified Web schema is format 23 (unchanged by W40.5). W40.5 protects the independent Device Save when account sign-in expires or the player changes accounts; local account copies can be loaded without signing in or uploading, and unsafe sign-out/replacement stops on stale or unreadable device data. W40.4 device upgrades keep the prior local recovery copy and cloud upgrades retain the exact previous cloud revision; large history and Fossil Archive records are stored compactly. Always export a Device JSON and a confirmed Cloud History backup before switching versions, moving accounts or importing data. Review stale-tab and invalid-save warnings. Older Web clients must not load format-23 campaigns. World/Desktop's local saves cannot be converted or merged.

Protected W27–W40.5 format migrations #

Web milestones progress through 8 → 9 → 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 → 14 → 15 → 16: W27.1 to W31 established formats 8–13; W31.1/W31.2 kept 13; W32.0 added Challenge Expedition records in format 14; W33.0 adds persistent five-floor dungeon runs in format 15, while W34.0 Dungeon Practice is simulation-only and retains format 15 with no migration; W34.1 changes only paid dungeon entry stamina and retains format 15, checkpoints, pending cargo and earned clears; W35.0 migrates format 15 → 16 to store bounded Ancient Hunt proof, active-entry/report and first-clear records without overwriting old Operations, Dungeon cargo, workshop queues or existing campaigns. Existing dungeon clears, attempts and best floors migrate intact, without back-paying earlier rewards. Completed reward cargo may remain pending until it can be collected safely, and reloading during an active dungeon fight permits retreat rather than replaying that floor. W36.0 keeps format 16 and indexes existing records without a migration or automatic reward collection. Never downgrade a format-16 save into an older Web client (and do not open format-15 saves in clients older than W33): export the Device Save and confirmed Cloud History revisions first, follow stale-tab warnings and keep recovery copies. World/Desktop has unrelated local profile files and no automatic Web account conversion.

W40.5 compatibility note: There is no Web save-format change beyond 23. Existing account saves, disconnected account copies and the separate device slot remain distinct across session loss, sign-out, cloud switching, explicit import and local recovery. If verification fails, download/export and inspect the current save instead of overwriting another slot. The W40.4 format-23 migration rules above still apply; World/Desktop schema 330 is unrelated.

Starting or reconnecting a cloud account #

  1. Open Player Account and sign in or create a Web account where available.
  2. Compare the current Device Save with the Cloud Save. If they differ, choose deliberately whether to load the Cloud Save or upload the Device Save.
  3. If prompted before loading cloud progress, review the warning. The current device campaign is retained separately and verified before replacement. Signing out returns you to device progress.
  4. Do not assume your cloud copy contains recent unsynced device changes. The Account status shows whether cloud syncing is connected and whether backup warnings need attention.

Download a save before major changes #

  • Legacy & Saves → Export Save: downloads the current device campaign as JSON.
  • Player Account → Download Device Save: also downloads the current device campaign separately from cloud history.
  • Account History → Download Saved Cloud Backup: downloads the last confirmed cloud revision; unsynced device-only changes are excluded.
  • Account History → Earlier revisions: download an older saved cloud version as JSON without restoring it.

Keep these downloaded JSON files somewhere safe before changing browsers, domains, devices or account saves. Import validates the file before replacing progress. Device-local storage belongs to a particular website address and browser; it does not automatically follow a site move.

Cloud history and rollback #

Cloud history can retain up to 10 previous revisions. Routine synchronization creates a new history recovery point no more often than about once every six hours, helping older restore points survive frequent autosaves. An explicit restore also preserves the save it displaces.

To restore an older cloud revision, open Account History, review the target and confirm. Cloud history restoration waits for the latest device progress and synchronisation checks before proceeding; saves/account changes are paused while it runs. The game verifies a local copy of your existing campaign first. If the copy cannot be confirmed, or a delayed save would conflict, recovery stops rather than replacing progress. Cloud restoring is not the same as simply downloading a backup.

Restore an earlier device campaign #

In Legacy & Saves, use the appropriate recovery panel:

  • Previous Save Before Last Replacement: review, download or restore the valid device save kept before your latest import or local account recovery. The replacement preserves the campaign you are leaving as the newest recovery copy. This action does not upload to or replace the cloud.
  • Cloud Restore Copies: review verified device snapshots kept before a cloud-history rollback; download them or restore one to this device. Restoring a device copy leaves cloud progress unchanged. Remove a saved copy only after downloading it, using the confirmation.
  • Device Recovery Copies: download preserved raw copies if the game encountered unreadable or unrecognised browser save data. Raw copies are for safekeeping and may not be valid imports.

A save import must first verify a copy of the current device save. If that backup fails, importing stops without replacing current progress. Never use Reset or repeated imports to bypass an unresolved storage/recovery warning.

Local account backup while offline #

If the cloud cannot be reached, the Account panel may offer Load Local Account Copy saved in this browser, or Restore an Unsynced Local Backup when signed in. Before applying it, the game shows available details such as Stage, Bones and saved time, confirms the current device campaign has a recovery copy, and checks that the source and device saves did not change during review. This loads progress on the device only; upload to Cloud later only if you choose to.

If the browser warns about saving #

  • A Device Save or Backup warning means the browser could not confirm a write or local recovery copy. Download the current save if offered; do not assume visible progress was safely persisted.
  • For an unreadable save, preserve and download the original raw data before continuing. The game may pause autosaving and cloud replacement until you resolve it.
  • For a save created by a newer version, update the Web game before importing or restoring it. Older clients block incompatible saves and cloud downgrades; an incompatible newer cloud backup may still be downloaded for safekeeping.
  • For a Save Conflict across tabs, the stale tab pauses device writes. Download that tab's progress if needed, then use Reload Latest Save. Avoid playing the same device campaign in multiple active tabs.
  • If a save has moved to another website address, export JSON on the old address and import it on the new address before deliberately uploading it to the correct cloud account.

Cloud, browser recovery and storage services can fail; keeping occasional external JSON backups is still recommended.

39. Recommended progression path #

Very early game — stages 1–50 #

Focus on:

  • keeping 3 active dinosaurs;
  • Growth;
  • first Talents;
  • basic crafted equipment;
  • Fossil Excavation / Foraging / Quarrying;
  • Fangwood Dungeon after Stage 10;
  • recruiting useful early species.

Do not spend all Shards immediately. Permanent mutations are valuable later.

Early-mid game — stages 50–250 #

Focus on:

  • filling all 3 gear slots on each active dinosaur;
  • getting gear to useful levels;
  • unlocking better gathering nodes;
  • starting Breeding;
  • upgrading Storehouse / Workshop / Nursery;
  • clearing stronger Dungeons;
  • working toward 75% Growth and Prime;
  • building alternate saved packs.

Late Tier 1 — stages 250–1,000 #

You should be developing:

  • full Levelled equipment;
  • Growth milestones;
  • Prime dinosaurs;
  • mutations;
  • stronger species;
  • Titans and biome mastery;
  • Workshop skill;
  • hatchlings/genes;
  • Weekly/Journey progress.

Try to enter Tier 2 with a stable three-dinosaur team rather than one overpowered dinosaur carrying two weak companions.

Tier 2 — stage 1,001+ #

This is where the major endgame loop begins.

Priorities:

  • Mutant Guardians;
  • set farming;
  • first-clear DNA;
  • Guardian Reconnaissance Sanctuary Missions;
  • Anomaly Watch Weekly Contract;
  • Tier 2 artifacts;
  • full 3-piece sets;
  • hybrid preparation.

Higher Expedition Tiers #

Continue replacing lower-tier gear with higher-tier versions.

Even if an old set gives the correct 3-piece bonus, later-tier pieces have dramatically larger raw stats.

Use:

  • Titan aura;
  • full Growth;
  • Prime;
  • Talents;
  • Mutations;
  • genes;
  • equipment sets;
  • higher-tier species/hybrids;
  • facilities;
  • skill Mastery;
  • Expedition Ascension.

40. If you are stuck on a stage #

Use this checklist in order.

1. Check active party #

Are all three active slots filled?

2. Check Pack Power #

Compare your Pack Power against enemy threat.

3. Check Growth #

Are your main dinosaurs far behind their current Growth potential?

4. Spend Talent Points #

Unused points provide no benefit.

5. Check equipment slots #

Does every dinosaur have Weapon + Armour + Relic?

6. Upgrade equipment #

Level 1 gear can often be made substantially better cheaply.

7. Use Auto Equip #

Then inspect manually.

8. Check set bonuses #

Do not accidentally break a good 2/3-piece set.

9. Consider mutations #

A good permanent mutation can be a major jump.

10. Awaken Prime #

At 75% Growth if you have Essence.

11. Improve team synergy #

A Tank + attacker or Support + Tank/Brawler can outperform three random high-stat dinosaurs.

12. Farm a better Guardian / Mutant #

Gear is often the bottleneck.

13. Fight Titans #

Unique Titan kills add permanent Attack aura.

14. Use Training Grounds #

Compare packs without spending anything.

41. Common mistakes #

Using only one strong dinosaur #

Pack systems reward having a proper team.

Ignoring reserve dinosaurs #

Reserves can run Sanctuary Missions while your main pack fights.

Farming normal enemies for gear #

Normal enemies only have a 24% gear chance. Cleared Guardians are more reliable.

Expecting sweeps to farm Shards #

Sweeps do not award repeat boss Mutation Shards. Manually replay bosses when Shards are the goal.

Breaking a full equipment set #

Use the comparison warning before replacing a piece.

Salvaging good gear accidentally #

Use gear locks.

Spending every Shard early #

Mutations are permanent and hybrids need Shards later.

Forgetting free Stamina recovery #

Use Daily Rest before paying for a tonic if appropriate.

Leaving gathering capped #

Gathering stops accumulating after 8 hours until collected.

Assuming breeding requires the same species #

Parents only need the same diet. Offspring is one of the two parent species.

Expecting breeding to make hybrids #

It cannot. Hybrids require Mutant DNA and the Genetic Lab.

Sending battle-party dinosaurs on missions #

Sanctuary Missions use reserve companions.

Forgetting Weekly reset #

Weekly rewards must be claimed before Monday 00:00 AEST (UTC+10).

Moving websites without a backup #

Export the JSON first.
