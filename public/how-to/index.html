Getting Started

Get onto Hollow Valley and connect the Steam account you play with.

  1. Switch to Evrima

    In Steam, right-click The Isle, open Properties → Betas (or Game Versions & Betas) and select Evrima. Let the download finish before opening the game.

  2. Find Hollow Valley

    Open the server browser, choose Unofficial Servers and search Hollow Valley. Join the Australian server on Gateway, choose a dinosaur and spawn.

  3. Connect your Steam account

    Open My Profile on the Hollow Valley website and choose Sign in with Steam. Use the same account you play with. You can link Discord from your profile too.

CHAPTER 2 OF 7

Dino Storage

Park a dinosaur and return to it later. Storage preserves species, growth, mutations, Prime state, skin and diet. All players have free, unlimited storage.

Park your active dinosaur
  1. Sign into the website with Steam and spawn on Hollow Valley.
  2. Open Dino Storage and choose Store Current In-Game Dino. Follow the requirements shown on the page.
  3. Wait for confirmation and check that the dinosaur appears in storage before leaving the game.
Redeem a stored dinosaur
  1. Spawn on the server as the stored dinosaur’s species. Match its sex if the storage card asks you to.
  2. Find the dinosaur in Dino Storage and choose Redeem when it becomes available.
  3. Confirm the request and check your dinosaur in-game after the restore finishes. The slot is used up after a successful restore.

If an action times out or the dinosaur is missing, check Dinosaur History and contact staff before retrying.

CHAPTER 3 OF 7

Live Map

Find your dinosaur on Gateway and use map layers to plan where to go next.

  1. Find your dinosaur

    Sign in with Steam, spawn on Hollow Valley and select MY DINO on the Live Map.

  2. Choose your layers

    Start with Fresh water and Landmarks. Turn on the Gateway grid, roads, caves, sanctuaries, patrol or migration zones when useful.

  3. Plan your next move

    On desktop, hold Shift and click to drop a private target pin. Check the Prime Tracker for your tracked progress.

Live positions refresh about every five seconds while the map tab is active and tracking is available. Static map layers are available during downtime.

Where do my rewards go?

Valley Coins (VC) are the server currency. Your Wallet shows your balance, verified playtime earnings and transactions. Some quests boost eligible future playtime earnings. Check your Wallet for any available daily bonus.

CHAPTER 7 OF 7

Valley Arcade

Play free challenges, earn crates and collect colour presets. Nothing in the Arcade is bought, staked or cashed out.

  1. Sign in with Steam

    Open Valley Arcade and choose Sign in with Steam. Use the same account you play with.

  2. Play a free challenge

    Choose Dino Trivia, the Daily Puzzle or Guess the Dino. A correct answer earns Arcade XP, a Valley fragment and Valley Coins. Free plays reset at midnight Brisbane time.

  3. Open what you earn

    Fragments combine into crates. Each crate shows its odds as rounded percentages. Membership never changes those odds. Dinosaur rewards can go to Dino Storage, and they do not replace the dinosaur you are playing. Emberwake, Frostveil and Verdant Warden are earned colour presets, not shop items.

Need a hand?

I can’t find the server

Check that Steam is running Evrima. Choose Unofficial Servers and search Hollow Valley again. The server may take a few minutes to reappear after a restart. Check server status.

My dinosaur isn’t on the Live Map

Check that you’re signed into the Steam account you play with and that your dinosaur has spawned on Hollow Valley. If tracking is unavailable, you can still use the static layers.

My dinosaur is missing from storage

Check Dinosaur History and contact staff before repeating a store or redeem action.

My Valley Coins haven’t updated

Check verified playtime and recent activity in your Wallet. Contact staff if your balance still looks wrong.
