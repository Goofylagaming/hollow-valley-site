## Accuracy policy

A high-detail mesh is not automatically an accurate EVRIMA skin preview.

Hollow Valley distinguishes:

- **shape** — realistic paint mesh is available, but EVRIMA colour-zone UV mapping is still being calibrated;
- **zones** — the mesh has a verified Hollow Valley zone mask and can display the individual EVRIMA/FNF-style channels;
- **pending** — no acceptable reusable paint mesh has been installed yet.

SkinInspector captures are used to compare the website preview against the actual live EVRIMA customizer values and mesh/material metadata.
